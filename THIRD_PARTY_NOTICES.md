**Needs owner review** (facts collected while preparing this file; no change was made to the items themselves):

1. **LPPL "part of a Work".** Only `IEEEtran.cls`/`IEEEtran.bst` of the IEEEtran bundle and only `tikz-cd.sty` of
   tikz-cd (its header lists `tikzlibrarycd.code.tex`, `tikz-cd.sty` and `tikz-cd-doc.tex` as the Work) are committed.
   LPPL 1.3c clause 2 treats distributing only part of a Work as a modification; clause 6(d)(2) lets a modified
   distribution instead carry information sufficient to obtain the complete, unmodified Work. The CTAN links in
   [Bundled assets](#bundled-assets) provide that information.
2. **IEEE starter template.** `IEEE_JOURNAL_MAIN_TEX` in `src/services/template_store.py` reuses the title, author and
   `\markboth` lines of `bare_jrnl.tex` from the IEEEtran bundle (LPPL 1.3) in shortened form. The IEEEtran headers ask
   that modified files be clearly marked as such.
3. **Springer LNCS starter template.** Several placeholder lines in `SPRINGER_LNCS_MAIN_TEX`
   (`src/services/template_builtins.py`) match Springer's LNCS `samplepaper.tex`. CTAN lists the `llncs` package as
   CC BY 4.0, and Overleaf lists the Springer proceedings template as CC BY 4.0, which asks for attribution and an
   indication of changes. **Resolved:** the LNCS starter is now labelled `CC BY 4.0 — adapted from Springer's LNCS
   sample paper (samplepaper.tex); shortened and reorganised into an IMRaD outline for Arionear` (`_LNCS_LICENSE`);
   registries seeded with the old LPPL label are corrected on start-up. The Elsevier starter keeps its LPPL label.
4. **Native libraries inside psycopg2-binary.** The Linux wheel bundles shared libraries in `psycopg2_binary.libs/`
   (`libpq`, `libssl`/`libcrypto`, `libkrb5`, `libgssapi_krb5`, `libk5crypto`, `libkrb5support`, `libcom_err`,
   `libldap`, `liblber`, `libsasl2`, `libselinux`, `libpcre`, `libkeyutils`, `libcrypt`) without their license texts.
   Their licenses: unknown — needs owner review. (Pillow and pypdfium2 ship the license texts of the libraries they
   bundle inside their wheels: Pillow in its `LICENSE` file, pypdfium2 under `licenses/data/*/BUILD_LICENSES/`.)
5. **Logos and avatar of unknown origin.** The provider/model logos and the assistant avatar listed under
   [Bundled assets](#bundled-assets) have no recorded source or license.
6. **Webfont conversion of Latin Modern.** The font license is known (GUST Font License, from the font files), but the
   origin of the `*-webfont.woff` conversions is not recorded.

## Frontend production dependencies

`dependencies` of [`frontend/package.json`](./frontend/package.json) and their transitive closure from
`frontend/package-lock.json`, listed with license-checker-rseidelsohn 4.4.2 (`--production --excludePrivatePackages`;
Linux optional binaries).

What actually ships is `frontend/dist/`: the browser bundle (`dist/client/`, including the pdf.js worker from
`pdfjs-dist`, Apache-2.0) and the Nitro node-server bundle (`dist/server/`), into which Vite/Nitro bundle the runtime
code of these packages (`dist/server/_libs/*.mjs`, plus `tslib` traced into `dist/server/node_modules/`). The table is a
superset: `dependencies` also holds build-time plugins (`@tailwindcss/vite`, `@tanstack/router-plugin`,
`vite-tsconfig-paths`, `tailwindcss`), so packages such as `typescript`, `lightningcss`, `@babel/*` and
`caniuse-lite` are listed although they only run during the build.

**Fonts inside `katex`.** `katex` is MIT, but the stylesheet `katex/dist/katex.min.css` (imported by
`frontend/src/lib/markdown-math-plugins.ts`) makes Vite copy the KaTeX fonts — 20 faces as `.woff2`/`.woff`/`.ttf` —
into `dist/client/assets/`. Their font name tables state OFL-1.1 with Reserved Font Names, © 2009–2010 Design Science,
Inc. and © 2014–2018 Khan Academy (same as the vendored copies under [Bundled assets](#bundled-assets)).

**Bundled from a devDependency.** The Nitro server runtime comes from `nitro` (a devDependency, MIT) and is therefore
not in the table or in the CI gate. Besides the packages above, the server bundle contains code from `h3`, `hookable`,
`ocache`, `ohash`, `unstorage`, `croner` and `unctx` (the last two vendored inside `nitro/dist/node_modules/`) — all MIT
according to their `package.json`.
